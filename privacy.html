Privacy policy

Field Awareness is used only by its owner, for personal use.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the tools read and write only the specific Google Drive files that the owner selected for them or that they created. They request no other Google data.

What it shares

No personal data is sold, shared or used for advertising. Weather information is fetched from public weather services. Optional notifications are sent only to the owner's own devices.

Retention and removal

Data stays in the owner's own Google Drive and devices. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
